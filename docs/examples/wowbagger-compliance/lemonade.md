Thank you for raising this initiative, and please pass on my thanks to the proposers. Considerable enthusiasm has been noted. Enthusiasm has been recorded on the risk register as a contributing factor.

1\. **Scope.** The proposal is understood to be the establishment, on municipal pedestrian infrastructure in the City of Boston, of an unincorporated retail concern for the point-of-sale distribution of a non-standardised acidified beverage by two operatives of undeclared age. The scope as described is insufficiently defined for review to commence. This is Finding 1.

2\. **Finding 2 – Unpermitted food establishment.** *105 CMR 590.000*, the State Sanitary Code Chapter X, adopts the FDA Food Code and requires a permit from the local board of health before any food establishment operates. Boston issues these through Inspectional Services. The Food Code treats **ice** as a food. It follows that the ice is a food, the ice requires a source, the source requires a permitted supply, and the cooler requires temperature logging. **Severity: High.** *Remediation:* permit application, a designated Person in Charge holding a Certified Food Protection Manager credential, and a documented allergen-awareness process per the Commonwealth's food allergy requirements.

3\. **Finding 3 – Unlicensed pedling.** *M.G.L. c. 101*, "Transient Vendors, Hawkers and Pedlers", requires a licence from the Division of Occupational Licensure for itinerant sale of goods. The spelling is the statute's, not mine. **Severity: Medium.**

4\. **Finding 4 – Obstruction of the accessible route.** The *Americans with Disabilities Act*, Title III, and the Access Board's *Public Right-of-Way Accessibility Guidelines* require a continuous pedestrian access route with a minimum 4 ft clear width. A card table is 30 in deep. A queue is not. **Severity: High.** *Remediation:* a Public Works occupancy permit, a certified site plan, and cones.

5\. **Finding 5 – Youth employment.** The *Fair Labor Standards Act* (29 U.S.C. §201 et seq.) restricts employment of minors under 14, subject to the parental-employment exemption; the Commonwealth separately requires youth employment permits under *M.G.L. c. 149*. Separately, the IRS requires any individual – including a nine-year-old – with net self-employment earnings of **$400 or more** to file Schedule SE. **Severity: High.** The sign is well drawn. I have kept a copy.

6\. **Finding 6 – Meals excise.** Sales of prepared beverages are subject to the 6.25% Massachusetts meals tax plus the 0.75% local option excise adopted by the City of Boston. Vendor registration with the Department of Revenue must precede the first sale. **Severity: Medium.**

7\. **Finding 7 – Container deposits.** The *Beverage Container Recovery Law*, M.G.L. c. 94 §§321–327, imposes a 5¢ deposit on **carbonated** beverages only. The submission does not state whether the product is carbonated. The Committee cannot therefore determine whether deposit obligations attach, and cannot approve on the assumption that they do not.

8\. **Finding 8 – Tidelands.** Much of Boston is filled tideland. Occupation of filled tidelands is licensed under *M.G.L. c. 91*, whose jurisdiction descends directly from the **Colonial Ordinance of 1641–1647**. It has never been repealed. A Chapter 91 determination is required before the table is unfolded.

9\. **Finding 9 – Day of operation.** *M.G.L. c. 136 §5* prohibits keeping open a place of business on the Lord's Day, excepting works of necessity and charity. The exemptions in §6 are extensive but are not self-executing on the facts presented. **Severity: To be determined by others.**

10\. **Procedural requirements.**\
10.1 Form ISD-114-B (Rev. 9), Notification of Intended Temporary Vend.\
10.2 The Board of Health application requires the **Public Works occupancy permit number** in Box 4c. Box 4c may not be left blank.\
10.3 A pre-application readiness screening must precede the application.\
10.4 The Cross-Functional Sidewalk & Beverage Sub-Committee sits quarterly.\
10.5 The Public Works occupancy permit requires **a valid food establishment permit number**, which is validated at issue.

11\. **Conditions for approval.** Certified site plan; potable water attestation; public liability cover naming the City; a labelled product with net quantity declared in accordance with weights-and-measures requirements; a signed statement of carbonation status; and such further information as the Committee may require.

12\. **Indicative timeline.** Papers circulate ten working days before the window. The deadline for the current cycle was 9 September. The Committee does not sit in August. The chair is on secondment until Q1 2027. An indicative determination date of Q3 2027 is achievable, though I would hate to commit to that.
