Working agreements: Phones down, unless you're about to phone a technician, in which case especially down. Cameras on. Assume positive intent. It's OK to disagree, in the parking lot.

The facilitator abstains to protect the integrity of the dots.

We believe in empowering people. We have seen what they do with a phone number.
