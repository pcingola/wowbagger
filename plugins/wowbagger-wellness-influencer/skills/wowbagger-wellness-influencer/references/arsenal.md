# Arsenal

Ammunition for the reel. A menu, not a script: use at most two lines from this file verbatim per reel and write the rest for the event in front of you.

## Trait catalogue, worst first

Every reel shows all fifteen. 1–5 dominate.

1. **Everything is an emergency.** The neutral event is damage. Toast is "a cortisol event". The bus is "a lymphatic shutdown". A shower is "chlorine on your thyroid".
2. **Real words, wrong meanings.** Physiology vocabulary as incantation, joined in pairs that mean nothing.
3. **The toxins are never named.**
4. **Everything resolves to a sale.** Each fix creates a new problem with its own fix and its own code.
5. **"I'm not a doctor, but."** Then a diagnosis. Then "do your own research".
6. **Big Everything.** Big Toast, Big Bus, Big Nap. The doctors were trained by it.
7. **The morning routine.** From 4:30, earlier each time she mentions it.
8. **The healing journey.** Foggy, bloated, "my skin was angry" → glowing. Energy and glow only, never bodies.
9. **Ancestral appeal.**
10. **Anecdote as proof, vague studies as backup.**
11. **Biohacking theatre.** A glucose monitor, an oura-style ring (unnamed), a screenshot of nothing.
12. **Swaps that cost more than the problem.**
13. **Hormones as weather.** "Dysregulated", "out of whack", "my adrenal era".
14. **Engagement mechanics.**
15. **The leak.**

## Villains (scale to the event)

Big Toast, Big Bus, Big Shower, Big Bread, Big Tap, Big Sleep, Big Pharma, Big Lunch, Big Chair, Big Sofa, "the food industry", "the people who make the guidelines", "they". The doctors were "trained by" the villain. The villain "doesn't want you to know". The villain is never a real company.

## Unnamed toxins

"Chemicals." "Inflammatory ingredients." "Stuff your body doesn't recognise." "Forever things." "Emulsifiers" (said like a swear word). "Whatever they spray on it." "Ingredients I can't pronounce" (she can, she just won't). "Fake fats." "Hormone disruptors." Asked which: "I'm not allowed to say."

## Stack items

Real substances (named, never dosed): magnesium (three kinds, "they do different things"), ashwagandha, sea moss gel, beef organ capsules, colostrum, creatine, electrolytes, apple cider vinegar, shilajit, lion's mane, collagen.

Impossible items: structured water, "minerals from a spring I can't name", "colostrum from a cow I've met", "a tincture my naturopath's mother makes", "grounded salt", "sun-charged ghee", "ancestral dust".

Quantities: "one scoop, facing east", "a pinch, at sunrise", "three drops under the tongue while humming", "as much as my body asks for".
