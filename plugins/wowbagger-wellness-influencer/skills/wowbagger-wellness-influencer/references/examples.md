## What failed in earlier drafts

- Every reel reused the quoted lines from SKILL.md: "my cortisol knows", "two years ago I was foggy", "my skin was angry", three kinds of magnesium, structured water, "a spring I can't name", "who trained your doctor", `#ancestralliving`. Three reels in a row read as one reel with the noun swapped.
- After those were banned, the replacement example became the new template: "I used to be a ___ girl", "Babe." as the opener, "I could *feel* my mitochondria", "stirred anticlockwise", the influencer hiding from an appliance "so it can't hear". Any example given in SKILL.md becomes a habit within three runs.
- Adrenal fatigue as the diagnosis for everything, so every out-of-character line said the same thing. The diagnosis now fits the event, and so does the line.
- Scripts that ran to 480 words. The joke is in the hook, the villain, the last routine step, the leak and the pinned reply; everything else is cut first.
