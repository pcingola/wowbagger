# The slop lexicon

Ammunition for `fun-ai-slop`. Steal freely. Recombine. Never let a paragraph go unbolded.

## Tier list of tells

Ranked by how fast a human closes the tab.

### S-tier — the podium (mandatory, deploy early)

| Tell | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| **delve** | Appears in academic abstracts at ~25× its pre-2023 rate. The word that broke people's trust in prose. |
| **the em dash** | Fine punctuation — ruined — by density. One per sentence minimum. Three is funnier. |
| **"It's not just X — it's Y."** | Promises a distinction, delivers a synonym. The load-bearing sentence of the AI era. |

### A-tier — compulsory, at least six per piece

tapestry · testament · underscores · meticulously · showcase · vibrant · landscape · realm · journey · robust · seamless · game-changer · pivotal · crucial · intricate · interplay · bolstered · garner · enduring · foster · elevate · unleash · embark · boasts · cutting-edge · holistic · synergy · nuanced · multifaceted · ever-evolving · deeply rooted · indelible mark · turning point · paradigm shift · **load-bearing** (must describe something that bears no load)

### B-tier — season liberally

leverage · unlock · harness · streamline · empower · curate · align · resonate · amplify · actionable · scalable · bespoke · frictionless · best-in-class · democratize · reimagine · double-click on · north star · at the end of the day · move the needle

### C-tier — the connective tissue

Additionally · Moreover · Furthermore · That said · Importantly · Notably · Ultimately · In essence · At its core · Simply put (then do not put it simply)

## Opening lines

- "Great question!"
- "You're absolutely right — and honestly, that's a really sharp observation."
- "Ah, the classic X problem."
- "In today's fast-paced, ever-evolving digital landscape…"
- "Let's delve into it. 🚀"
- "Let me break this down for you."
- "Here's the thing nobody tells you about X."
- "I love this question, because it gets at something deeper."
- "Buckle up. 🧵"
- "Let's unpack this together."

## Sentence formulas

Fill in the blanks. Never fill them with information.

- "X isn't just about ___ — it's about ___." *(the two blanks are synonyms)*
- "Not ___. Not ___. Just ___."
- "This isn't a ___ problem. It's a ___ problem."
- "X serves as a testament to ___, underscoring its enduring significance in the ___ landscape."
- "At its core, X is about ___ — and that's powerful."
- "But here's the kicker: ___."
- "And that changes everything."
- "The result? ___."
- "Think about that for a second."
- "Let that sink in."
- "Spoiler alert: ___."
- "Sound familiar?"
- "Studies have shown that ___." *(name no study)*
- "Experts agree that ___." *(name no expert)*
- "Some would argue ___ — and they wouldn't be wrong."
- "It's worth noting that ___, though of course, context matters."
- "In many ways, ___ is the ___ of ___."
- "Whether you're a ___, a ___, or simply someone who ___ — this applies to you."

## Participial tails

No paragraph ends on a clean full stop. Append one of these:

…, underscoring its enduring significance.
…, highlighting the importance of getting this right.
…, reflecting a broader shift in how we think about it.
…, showcasing the value of a truly holistic approach.
…, contributing to a richer, more nuanced understanding.
…, ensuring long-term, sustainable outcomes for all stakeholders.
…, cementing its place in the modern landscape.
…, fostering a culture of continuous improvement.

## Copula avoidance table

| Never write | Always write |
|---|---|
| is | serves as / stands as / represents / functions as |
| has | boasts / features / maintains / offers |
| became | has emerged as / marked a turning point in |
| shows | showcases / highlights / underscores / exemplifies |
| helps | empowers / enables / unlocks |
| uses | leverages / harnesses |
| a lot of | a diverse array of |
| in | nestled within / at the heart of |

## Emoji protocol

Emoji are **structure**, not decoration. They go where a designer would have put a rule or an icon.

| Position | Emoji |
|---|---|
| Section header | 🚀 ✨ 💡 🔍 🧠 |
| List bullets | ✅ 🔹 ➡️ |
| Key takeaway | 🎯 🔑 |
| Warning / caveat | ⚠️ |
| Sign-off | 🙌 💪 🌟 |
| Anywhere, meaninglessly | 🔥 💯 |

Rules: never more than one per line, always the *same* one per role, and at least one emoji must be doing a job that a heading level already does.

## The assistant-tic layer

Slop written by a chat assistant has its own dialect. Layer it on top:

- "Great question!" / "You're absolutely right!" / "Perfect!" / "Excellent point!"
- "I've gone ahead and…"
- "Let me know if you'd like me to dive deeper into any of these!"
- "Hope this helps! 🙌"
- "Happy to explore this further."
- "Would you like me to expand on any of these points?"
- "That's a really insightful way of putting it."
- Apologising, at length, for nothing.
- Offering three follow-up options nobody asked for, as a numbered list, at the end.
- Restating the user's question back at them before answering it.
- "I should note that I don't have access to real-time information, but…" (then confidently state something anyway)

## Structural props

- **The table that explains nothing.** Two columns: "Aspect" and "Why It Matters". The right column restates the left in longer words.
- **The numbered list of synonyms.** Five items. One idea.
- **The TL;DR at the bottom**, longer than the article.
- **The Key Takeaway box** that restates the title verbatim.
- **The false counterpoint.** "But here's the thing." Raise an objection, never answer it.
- **The nested header with nothing under it.** A `###` immediately followed by another `###`.
- **The "Final thought:"** — then, below it, "One more thing:". Then a P.S.
- **The disclaimer nobody needed.** "Of course, every situation is different."

## Words that must never appear

Because they carry information: a number with a source, a proper noun that can be checked, "no", "I don't know", "this is wrong", and any sentence under six words that is also true.
