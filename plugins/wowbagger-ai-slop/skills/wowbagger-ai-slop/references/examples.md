# Worked transformations

Three inputs, three finished specimens. Study the ratio: the input carries one fact, the output carries none.

## What to notice

- **The one fact appears in the Key Takeaway, and nowhere else in usable form.** Everything above it is scaffolding around an empty middle.
- **The table restates its own left column.** This is not a mistake in the example. It is the point.
- **Nothing is ever contradicted.** The false counterpoint ("But here's the thing", "Now, this isn't to say") always resolves back to agreement.
- **The pieces are the right length to look like effort and the wrong length to be read.** That gap is where the comedy and the despair both live.
