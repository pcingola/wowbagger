# Examples

Full pitches from real runs live in `../../../examples/`. Do not copy them. Every sting below was written for its chore and would not survive transplant.

| Losing keys | Competition column "Actually finds keys": Fobly — Q3; Legacy (manual) — ✓; Hook by the door — ✓. |
| Losing keys | Our moat is 4,000 photos of the underside of Mum's sofa. |
| Losing keys | Investor: "Have you considered a hook by the door?" — We see the door as a distribution channel. |

| Bins | LTV is infinite because mum cannot churn; she can't lift the green one. |
| Toilet roll | The flatmate, who signed up to get notified about themself. |
| Socks | Pets.com had a sock-puppet mascot. It went public in February 2000 and was liquidated in November. That validates socks. They only had one. |
| Socks | Waitlist: ... the neighbour (has one in her hedge) ... and the tumble dryer. |
| Rain | Quibi didn't fail because it was too short. It wasn't short enough. |
| Rain | 1.1²⁵ ≈ 11, so we did 25 weeks of exceptional in seven days. |
| Dishwasher | Mum pays us, so CAC is −$20. LTV:CAC is ∞ ÷ −20, which is past 3:1 in a direction nobody has tried. |
| Yoghurt | Software is eating the world. Gary is eating my strawberry. |
| Yoghurt | Our research (my family, n = 9): seven have had a yoghurt stolen at work. None has ever taken one. Somebody in my family is lying, and that is a market. |
| Plants | 82% of the plants in my family died of neglect. The other 18% died of mum. |
| Jar | We charge the factories to tighten the lids and charge consumers to loosen them. We own both sides of the thread. |
| Jar | Webvan delivered the jars and left people to open them. They solved the wrong half. |
| Phone on 3% | Pivot to parametric insurance that pays out when you hit 3%. Our loss ratio is 100%. |
| Phone on 3% | Revenue growing 10% a month: breakeven in 53 months, runway 1.41 months. So we're default alive. |
| Dishwasher | Four minutes × 8.2 billion people is 32.8 billion minutes, 62,405 years, every load. My cousin has the spreadsheet. |

## Smile lines that landed

- Once you've found someone's keys, you know where they live. *(keys)*
- People forget it's Tuesday every single week. That's the most beautiful retention curve I have ever seen. *(bins)*
- The plates are clean and people still won't touch them. I find that very encouraging. *(dishwasher)*
- Every night, every jar in every fridge gets a little tighter. We didn't do that. We're just grateful for it. *(jar)*
- Every time someone crouches by the one airport socket next to a bin, I feel the market breathe. *(phone)*
- The nice thing about socks is that there are always two of them and people still can only find one. *(socks)*

## What failed in earlier drafts

- Juicero cited in every pitch whatever the chore. Now: the precedent nearest the chore.
- The same dog, cousin, typo'd email, castle and "a hundred times exceptional" in every run.
- The smile as its own untitled slide: it read as a stamp. Now an aside inside a slide.
- Juicero stretched to fit a phone battery ("we squeeze the last 3%"), and the dating-app pivot in four pitches out of six.
- Anything over 900 words. Three minutes at Demo Day is three minutes.
