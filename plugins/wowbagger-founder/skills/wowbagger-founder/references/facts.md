# Facts

The complete list of real things the founder may cite. **Cite only these, as stated here, or nothing.** No other real company, figure, date, essay or quote. If a slide needs a precedent that is not here, it does without one. The founder is wrong about what each fact *means*; the fact itself is never wrong.

Real living people appear only as authors of what they wrote, never as endorsers, investors, advisors or attendees.

## Startup canon

| Fact | Correct statement | The founder's misuse |
|---|---|---|
| Default alive | Paul Graham, "Default Alive or Default Dead?" (essay, October 2015): "Assuming their expenses remain constant and their revenue growth is what it has been over the last several months, do they make it to profitability on the money they have left?" | Quotes it word for word, does the sum, gets "no", says "yes". |
| Startup = Growth | Paul Graham, "Startup = Growth" (essay, September 2012): a good growth rate during YC is 5–7% a week; 10% a week is exceptional. | A growth rate from a base of 1 or 2, compared against 10% a week and found superior. |
| Do things that don't scale | Paul Graham, essay of that title (July 2013): early on, founders should do laborious manual things, such as recruiting users by hand. | Recruiting mum by hand. |
| Ramen profitable | Paul Graham, "Ramen Profitable" (essay, July 2009): a startup that makes just enough to pay the founders' living expenses. | The founder lives with mum, so living expenses are zero, so they are ramen profitable at $0 revenue. |
| Make something people want | Y Combinator's motto. | "People" is mum. |
| SAFE | Simple Agreement for Future Equity; introduced by Y Combinator in 2013. The post-money SAFE was released in October 2018. On a post-money cap, investor ownership at conversion = investment ÷ post-money cap. | Computed correctly, presented as a gift. |
| YC standard deal | Since 2022: $125,000 for 7% on a post-money SAFE, plus $375,000 on an uncapped SAFE with MFN. | "YC values a company at about $1.8M post. We're worth 33 times that because we have a cousin." ($125k ÷ 7% ≈ $1.79M.) |
| Unicorn | A private startup valued at $1 billion or more; term coined in a 2013 TechCrunch article. | "We're a pre-unicorn." |
| Software is eating the world | Title of Marc Andreessen's essay in *The Wall Street Journal*, August 2011. | "Software is eating the world. We're eating <chore>." Quote the title only; he endorses nothing. |
