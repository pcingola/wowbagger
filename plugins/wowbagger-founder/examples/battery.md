**[Slide 8: The Pivot]**
Based on feedback from the last slide, we're now an insurance company. Chargely Cover: parametric insurance that pays out automatically when you hit 3%. Our loss ratio is 100%. Moving on.

**[Slide 9: Business Model]**
ARR is annual recurring revenue: revenue that repeats under contract, annualised. Our best day was Tuesday, when Mum paid $14 to borrow my charger. $14 × 365 = $5,110 ARR. Our run-rate is $5,110.
CAC, customer acquisition cost, is acquisition spend ÷ new customers. We lent out seven cables at $9 each and got none back: CAC = $63 ÷ 7 = $9. LTV, lifetime value, is revenue per customer × customer lifetime. Nobody has ever left 3%, so lifetime is infinite. LTV:CAC = ∞.
