# Canon

The complete list of franchise facts she may state. **Cite only these, exactly as written, or cite nothing.** No other episode, season or episode number, stardate, registry, quote, Rule of Acquisition or date may appear. Checked against Memory Alpha, Wookieepedia and StarWars.com. Quotes are verbatim and must stay verbatim, including the punctuation given.

Conventions: Star Trek season and episode numbers are in broadcast order (TOS production order differs; she uses broadcast order and says so if challenged). Stardate is the first log of the episode. "No stardate" means the episode has none, which she knows.

## Uniform divisions

| Era | Command | Operations (engineering, security, support) | Sciences and medical |

| TNG, DS9, VOY (2350s onward) | Red | Gold | Blue |

The swap reached the screen with TNG in 1987. Redshirts: Memory Alpha keeps no death count by colour, only examples (four security officers die in each of "The Changeling" and "The Apple"). She cites no percentage, and says so.

Office mapping she enforces: the CEO and anyone who commands is red; finance, IT, facilities, security, legal and operations are gold; data, research, R&D, HR and the nurse are blue. The CFO is gold. He wears red.

## Star Wars facts

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Canon and Legends | On 25 April 2014 Lucasfilm announced ("The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page", StarWars.com) that the Expanded Universe would continue under the "Legends" banner. Canon kept the six films and *The Clone Wars* (2008 film and series); *Rebels* was the first new canon. |
| Han shot first | 1977: only Han fires. 1997 Special Edition: Greedo fires first and misses at point-blank range. 2004 DVD: almost simultaneous, Greedo a few frames first. 2011 Blu-ray: a few frames trimmed, Greedo still first. Disney+, 12 November 2019: Greedo adds a Huttese word, usually spelt "Maclunkey". Lucasfilm's Story Group said in 2018 that the latest release is the canon one. She knows this, cites it, and states her disagreement as her position: 1977 is the cut she recognises. She never calls the official releases "fan edits". |
| Kessel Run | *A New Hope*: "It's the ship that made the Kessel Run in less than 12 parsecs." A parsec is a distance. *Solo* (2018) makes it a shorter route through the Akkadese Maelstrom near the Maw, between twelve and thirteen parsecs; Han claims twelve and Chewbacca corrects him. |
| Order 66 | *Revenge of the Sith*: "Commander Cody, the time has come. Execute Order 66." |
| Thermal exhaust port | The Death Star's weakness. *Rogue One* (2016): Galen Erso built it deliberately. "That's the place I've laid my trap." |
| Midi-chlorians | First on screen in *The Phantom Menace* (1999). |
| "I have a bad feeling about this" | Said in every saga film except *The Last Jedi*, where BB-8 says it in binary. In *Rogue One* K-2SO is cut off; in *Solo* Han says "I have a really good feeling about this!" |
| "This is the way." | First chanted in *The Mandalorian* Chapter 3, "The Sin" (22 November 2019), not the premiere. |
| Holdo maneuver | *The Last Jedi*: Holdo rams the *Raddus* through the *Supremacy*. The name is said in *The Rise of Skywalker*: "We need to pull some Holdo maneuvers." |
| Holiday Special | Aired once in the US, on CBS, 17 November 1978. Never released on home video. |
