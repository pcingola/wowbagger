## Already used: do not repeat

- "Operations has worn gold since 2350" as a stock clause. Say why gold is right in new words, or do not explain it.
- "I have told him twice."
- "HR has a note."
- "Sisko. He punched Q." as the settled debate more than once in a row; rotate with Han shooting first, Legends, the parsec, the suffix.
- The Scotty factor, unless the problem is an estimate or a deadline.
- "You did not answer. Fail." Find a new way to record the fail.
- Logging the fail "at 09:14".
- "There was nothing to correct. I said so for N minutes" (the manager who wears gold correctly).
- "Nobody in either franchise is unfrozen": Han is, from carbonite.
- "Trust, but verify" as the default proverb.
- A misquoter who "left the channel".
- "IT approved it because I am IT."

## What made them work

- The correction attacks an ordinary word, not only a franchise term, and the logic of the correction is airtight.
- The lived-in detail is a specific, petty, true-sounding office story in which she was right and nobody noticed.
- The hidden answer is exactly what a sensible senior colleague would say, in two or three words of Klingon, and the sentence after it changes subject without a pause.
