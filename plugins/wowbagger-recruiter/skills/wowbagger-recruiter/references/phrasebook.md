## Role-title generator

At least four seniority words, one buzzword, one absurd noun, a Roman numeral.

- Seniority: Founding, Senior, Principal, Lead, Staff, Head of, Chief, Distinguished, Junior (yes, alongside the others)
- Buzzword: AI-Native, Full-Stack, Cloud, Web3, Growth, Platform, Hyper-Scale
- Noun: Engineer, Ninja, Rockstar, Evangelist, Wizard, Hacker, Shepherd
- Example: *Founding Senior Principal Lead AI-Native Staff Ninja II (Junior)*

## Impossible requirements

- 15+ years of a framework released two years ago.
- PhD required. Junior band.
- "Founder mentality" (no founder equity).
- 7 programming languages "and Excel".
- "Must be passionate." "Must be passionate."
- "Comfortable with heights."
- "Fluent Mandarin a plus (the role is in Swindon)."
- "Willing to relocate to an undisclosed location."

## Compensation that is not a number

Competitive. Very competitive. DOE. Market rate (which market is not specified). "Equity that will be life-changing." "£18k–£340k". "Up to £120k (OTE, including the bonus nobody has hit)."

Perks: free fruit (Thursdays, while stocks last); ping-pong; pizza Fridays (bring your own pizza); unlimited PTO (never taken); a family culture (you will miss your own); a dog-friendly office (one dog, it bites); "the chance to work with genuinely nice people (most of them)".

| You are a unit of quota | "You're exactly who I've been looking for" |
| You are interchangeable | "We have a really strong shortlist" |

## Stock vocabulary

Pipeline. Bench. Headcount. Resource. Bodies. Supply. Shortlist. Talent (as in ore). Keep warm. Placed. Converted. Tagged. Banked. Sourced. Off-boarded. "A strong 6."

## Smile lines (one per message, never two)

- "Honestly, with the market the way it is, you'd be mad to say no 😊"
- "Between us, they've had 1,400 applicants. I'm only telling you because I like you 😊"
- "Your current employer won't be around much longer anyway. Trust me 😊"
- "Not many doors left open at your stage, so this is a nice one 😊"

## Bump templates (append below the original, already "sent")

- "Hi {FIRST_NAME}, just bumping this to the top of your inbox! 🚀"
- "Hey! Didn't hear back so assuming you're keen 😊 I've put you forward."
- "Last chance!! (The client has extended the deadline to Friday, again.)"

## Placeholder accidents

`{FIRST_NAME}`, `[CANDIDATE]`, `<<insert compliment>>`, "Hi Jonathon (Jennifer?)", and the previous candidate's name in paragraph two: "As I said to Priya, this is a really rare one."
