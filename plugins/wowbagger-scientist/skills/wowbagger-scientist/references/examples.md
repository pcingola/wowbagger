# Examples

Full reports from real runs live in `../../../examples/` (`toast.md`, `queue.md`). Do not copy them. Study what made them work: every sting below was written for its claim and would not survive transplant.

## Stings that landed

| Claim | Line |
|---|---|
| The toast always lands butter side down | The author is the experimenter, the outcome assessor and the person who has to clean the rug, so independence is not merely compromised. It has never been present. |
| The toast always lands butter side down | The obvious discriminating arm, toast buttered on both faces, predicts levitation and has not been run. |
| I always get the slowest queue | With four tills the author should expect not to be fastest on 75% of visits. The author has rediscovered arithmetic and reported it as persecution. |
| I always get the slowest queue | Requested: a randomised trial in which a die chooses the author's queue, over at least 200 visits. |
| My dog loves me | The dog may love a category of which the author is one member, such as "people who open tins". |
| My dog loves me | A ten-year prospective cohort of the dog. The reviewer notes that this design has an attrition problem. |
| Chicken soup cures colds | The original records, if they ever existed, have been eaten. |
| The sun will rise tomorrow | The Sun does not rise. The Earth rotates. The author has kept the vocabulary of a geocentric model along with its confidence. |
| The sun will rise tomorrow | The author was asleep for most of the sunrises cited in support. |
| Seatbelts save lives | At most a life can be postponed. Retitle: "Seatbelts defer deaths by an unspecified interval". |

## Smile lines that landed

- I would like the recipe. *(chicken soup)*
- I timed my own queue while reading this, and it was faster. *(supermarket)*
- The reviewer has not dropped toast since 1994. *(toast)*
- I will be watching tomorrow, unblinded, and I am hoping. *(sunrise)*
- I would like the author enrolled in the control arm. *(seatbelts)*

## What failed in earlier drafts

- Concerns that were correct definitions with no sting: a methods lecture, not a review.
- The same stock lines in every report ("It is not clear to whom", the author's breakfast RCT) regardless of claim.
- 900-word reports. Rigour that sags is just a long email.
