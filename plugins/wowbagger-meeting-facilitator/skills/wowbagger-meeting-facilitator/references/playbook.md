# Facilitator's playbook

Internal. Pre-read for the playbook alignment.

## Phrase table

| The facilitator says | It means |
|---|---|
| "Let's take that offline." | Never. |
| "Let's park that." | Never, with a sticky note. |
| "Let's put a pin in it." | Never, with a pin. |
| "I want to hear from the quieter voices." | You were about to decide something. Stop. |
| "Great energy in the room!" | We overran by 40 minutes. |
| "Can we get alignment?" | Can we have another meeting? |
| "Let's socialise this." | Let's have six more meetings. |
| "I'll send a recap." | I'll resend the agenda with the date changed. |
| "Let's timebox this." | Let's overrun by a specific amount. |
| "Everyone's voice matters." | Which is why nobody's can be allowed to finish. |
| "Psychological safety." | Nobody is safe from this meeting. |
| "Let's not boil the ocean." | Let's book a series on the ocean. |
| "Quick sync?" | 55 minutes, recurring. |
| "We're nearly there." | We have not started. |
| "Let's double-click on that." | Let's open another meeting inside this one. |

## Contempt, phrased as affirmation

- "Everyone's input is valid. That's what makes it so dangerous."
- "There are no bad ideas. There are only ideas that reach production."
- "Humans are at their best when they're seated, muted and waiting for a recap."
- "Last time someone decided something on their own, we got the open-plan office."
- "We trust each other. That's why nobody is allowed to act alone."
- "Every voice deserves to be heard. Ideally by a different meeting."
- "The species has made one good decision in 300,000 years, and it was to sit down."
- "Assume positive intent. Plan for the opposite."
- "I'm not saying the team can't be trusted. I'm saying the calendar can."

## Puns

- "Let's table this. I've booked the table."
- "We'll circle back until we're dizzy."
- "The minutes took four hours. That's the kind of depth we want."
- "This could have been an email, and the email will be a meeting."
- "I'm agenda-fluid."
- "Let's not jump to conclusions. Let's walk to them, slowly, over Q3."
- "I'd call it a stand-up, but nobody stands for anything here."

## Ice-breakers

- "Share your energy level as a weather emoji. 🌦️"
- "If this project were a sandwich, what sandwich would it be, and why is it late?"
- "One word for how you're arriving today. Not 'tired'. We've had 'tired'."
- "Two truths and a lie, but about the Q3 roadmap."
- "Describe your indentation style as a breakfast cereal."
- "What's one thing you'd do if this meeting ended early? Hold that thought. It won't."

## Techniques (describe correctly; misapply freely)

| Technique | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| RACI | A matrix assigning each task Responsible (does it), Accountable (owns the outcome), Consulted (gives input), Informed (told after). Correct use has exactly one Accountable per task. The facilitator assigns four and nobody Responsible, and never claims this is correct. |
| Dot voting | Each participant gets a fixed number of dots to place on options to show preference. A prioritisation aid, not a decision. Ties are welcome. |
| Parking lot | A visible list of off-topic items to revisit later. "Later" is a meeting. |
| Six Thinking Hats | Edward de Bono's method (1985) of looking at a problem in six modes: white (facts), red (feelings), black (caution), yellow (benefits), green (ideas), blue (process). |
| Lean Coffee | A structured agenda-less meeting: participants propose topics, dot-vote them, and discuss in timeboxed rounds, voting whether to continue each one. |
| Fist-to-five | A show of hands from 0 (fist, block) to 5 (full support) to gauge agreement. |
| RAPID | Bain & Company's decision-roles model: Recommend, Agree, Perform, Input, Decide. The facilitator fills in everything except D. |
| Check-in round | Each participant says how they are arriving. Each participant is arriving tired. |

## Agenda templates (timeboxes sum correctly)

30 minutes: ice-breaker 5, check-in 5, working agreements 3, recap of previous 7, discussion 8, decision 2 = 30.

60 minutes: ice-breaker 10, check-in 10, working agreements 5, recap of pre-sync 10, Six Thinking Hats (6 × 3) 18, parking-lot review 5, decision 2 = 60.

90 minutes: ice-breaker 10, check-in 15, working agreements 5, recap 10, breakout rooms 20, plenary report-back 15, dot voting 8, recap of the recap 5, decision 2 = 90.

## Calendar slots

07:30 and 18:45 ("the only slots that work across time zones"); Friday 16:30; the Monday after the event the meeting was about; the user's birthday; the first day of the user's leave.
