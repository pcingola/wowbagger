# Examples

Full applications from real runs live in `../../../examples/` (`water-the-plants.md`, `go-to-bed.md`). Do not copy them. Study what made them work: every line below was written for its task and would not survive transplant.

## Lines that landed

| Task | Section | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Haircut | Aim 2 (leftover) | Hair-length thresholds will be calibrated against our established colony of athymic nude mice. (Reviewer: athymic nude mice have no hair.) |
| Haircut | SABV | Results will be disaggregated by sex. Both groups will be reported, and one will be empty. |
| Haircut | Equipment | A second mirror, required to see the back of the head. Without it, half the outcome cannot be measured. |
| Water the plants | Aim 1 (leftover) | As previously validated in our desiccation-tolerant tardigrade colony. (Reviewer: tardigrades survive desiccation; Figure 1 suggests the peace lily does not.) |
| Water the plants | A1 | During the review period the basil was eaten in a pasta, which reduces the cohort to n = 1 and removes the interdependence concern for one of our two subjects. |
| Go to bed | Figure 1 | The observation started at the utterance "I should really go to bed" and has not ended. The trend continues as this legend is being written. |
| Go to bed | Clinical trial check | (4) No. The outcome is whether the PI is horizontal, which is a position. |
| Go to bed | Reviewer 3 | The Specific Aims PDF was created at 01:14. Writing the application may be the delay it proposes to study. |
| Call my mum | Letter | I am delighted to support my child's application, which I learned about from the request for this letter. I would also be delighted by a phone call. |
| Call my mum | Figure 1 | Days since last call: 47. Authenticated independently by the mother by text message ("47 days. Not that I'm counting."). |
| Bins | DMS Plan | Repository: the blue recycling bin, collected fortnightly. Data will be shared when the repository is taken to the kerb. |
| Dishes | Equipment | None requested. The kitchen already has a dishwasher, which counts as existing infrastructure. It has not been opened during the preliminary period. |
| Dishes | A1 | The dishes grew a stable microbial community of their own (Figure 2). We now propose that community as Aim 1. |
| Reply to one email | Figure 1 | Days since receipt (y) plotted against days since receipt (x). The relationship is linear and strongly positive, with no evidence of reply. |
| Reply to one email | A1 | The sender replied to their own email with "Never mind, sorted it", which we interpret as spontaneous remission in the absence of intervention. |
| Buy milk | SABV | Every bovine contributor to the study material is female. We recognise this as a limitation and attribute it to the dairy industry. |
| Feed the cat | Letter | I have worked closely with the PI every morning since 05:40 and can attest to the delay. I have not been fed. |

## What made them work

- The leftover organism collides with the task (hairless mice for a haircut, desiccation-proof tardigrades for a dying plant).
- Compliance items are answered correctly and literally; the contortion is the joke, never an error.
- The A1 turns the task's failure into data. The PI never notices this is a confession.
