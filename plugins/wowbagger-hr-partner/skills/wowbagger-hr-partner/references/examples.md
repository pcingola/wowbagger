## "My manager scheduled a meeting at 7am on a Saturday"

**Subject:** Quick touchpoint re: weekend energy 💛✨

Hi there!

First, thank you for your courage in raising this, and for bringing your whole self to work. That includes the part of you that sleeps until 7am on Saturdays, which we see and value 💛.

I'm hearing that you experienced a calendar invitation. I understand a meeting has been scheduled at a time that sits outside some traditional definitions of the working week. Another way to see it is as an early-access, low-distraction collaboration window, offered by a senior stakeholder who clearly believes in you enough to want you there before anyone else is awake ✨.

It may help to look at this through Maslow's hierarchy of needs. Maslow placed physiological needs such as sleep, food and rest at the base of the pyramid and self-actualisation at the top. A Saturday 7am meeting is simply an invitation to spend less time at the base and more time near the top 💛.

This is a great moment to live our value of FLEXIBILITY. Flexibility belongs to all of us, and this weekend it belongs to you ✨.

To support you on this journey, I've put together a few next steps 💛:

1. Accept the invitation by **tonight, 21:00**, so the senior stakeholder feels supported.

Thank you for your courage in raising this, and for bringing your whole self to work, including the part of you that needs coffee. ✨

I'm hearing that, for approximately three weeks, you experienced a machine not producing a beverage. I want to sit with that. It sounds like the machine is on a journey of its own, and has chosen, for now, to hold space instead of hot water. ✨

You may find the Kübler-Ross model helpful: denial, anger, bargaining, depression, acceptance. At three weeks most colleagues have reached bargaining (the kettle on floor 4). I'd love to support you through to acceptance, which is where we'd like the whole floor to be by Q1. 💛

This is a real moment for OWNERSHIP. A machine nobody owns is a machine nobody can be blamed for, and I'd hate for that to be you. The machine itself has been nominated for our *Steady Presence* award, as it hasn't raised a single concern. We're a family, and families share a kettle. ✨
